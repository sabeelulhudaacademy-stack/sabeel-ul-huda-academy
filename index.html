About the Founder

Mufti Awais Rahman Jadoon — Scholar, Researcher, Teacher, Author

Founder & CEO

Mufti Awais Rahman Jadoon

Mufti Awais Rahman Jadoon is an Islamic Scholar, Researcher, Teacher, Author, and Founder of Sabeel ul Huda Academy. He is pursuing an M.Phil in Islamic Studies and has completed Dars-e-Nizami with specialization in Fiqh & Ifta. He has extensive experience in teaching Islamic sciences, academic research, thesis writing, and Islamic publications. His mission is to provide authentic Quranic and Islamic education through modern technology while nurturing knowledgeable, practicing, and morally upright Muslims around the world.

Zakat Calculator

Estimate based on the silver Nisab (612.36g). Enter current market prices.

⚠️ This is a simplified estimate for guidance only, not a religious ruling (fatwa). Please confirm your Zakat calculation with a qualified scholar before paying.

Inheritance Calculator

Simplified estimate for common cases only (Quran 4:11–12).

⚠️ This is a simplified estimate covering common cases only — it does not account for siblings, grandchildren, or other heirs (Ilm-ul-Mirath has many detailed rules). Always confirm actual shares with a qualified Islamic scholar or mufti before distributing an estate.
